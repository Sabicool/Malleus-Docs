# Referencing

!!! warning
    Every card needs an **accepted source with a URL and access date**, or it will not be accepted. **Never copy text verbatim** — it risks automatic rejection without explanation. Can't find a source? Email [president@malleus.org.au](mailto:president@malleus.org.au).

## How to cite

Put at least one accepted source in the Source field, with a URL and a last-updated/accessed statement:

!!! example
    [Myocardial Infarction](https://next.amboss.com/us/article/wS0hbf) - AMBOSS - Last Updated August 20, 2025. Accessed September 18, 2025

!!! example
    Hepatitis B [published 2020 Dec; amended 2021 Mar]. In: Therapeutic Guidelines. Melbourne: Therapeutic Guidelines Limited; accessed 2025 Jan. [Link.](https://app.tg.org.au)

For books without a URL, give the [DOI](https://www.doi.org); if none exists: *Chapter 13 - Talley, O'Connor (2017) Clinical Examination: A Systematic Guide to Physical Diagnosis, Elsevier.* (no access date needed for published works). Consider AMA/Vancouver style ([guide](https://libguides.jcu.edu.au/ama)).

**Multiple sources:** list as bullets, mark one as primary, and prefix the others with 'Also see:'.

??? note "Creating hyperlinks in Anki"
    Use [this add-on](https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/318752047), or open the HTML editor (`< >`) and write `<a href="URL">Title</a> - RESOURCE - Last Updated … Accessed [date]`

## Accepted sources

See [Recommended Resources](https://www.notion.so/2595964e68a481898ccfebd84716168e) for the full list. In brief:

- [AMBOSS](https://next.amboss.com/us)* — link the specific article
- [PassMedicine](https://www.passmedicine.com)* questions
- [StatPearls](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK430685/)
- [Therapeutic Guidelines](https://app.tg.org.au) (see [eTG tagging](tagging.md#special-tags))
- Specialised: [DermNet](https://dermnetnz.org) (dermatology), [LITFL](https://litfl.com) (critical care), [Radiopaedia](https://radiopaedia.org) (radiology)
- Selected textbooks: Oxford Handbooks; Talley & O'Connor's *Clinical Examination* 8e; Llewellyn-Jones *Fundamentals of Obstetrics and Gynaecology*; Ellis & Calne's *Lecture Notes in General Surgery* 14e; First Aid 2024 (not for management)
- Society guidelines (RANZCOG, RACGP…) with website links

!!! warning
    *AMBOSS and PassMedicine cards on **investigations or management** must be independently checked against local Australian guidelines.

## Unacceptable sources

First Aid 2023 or older · Pathoma · Boards & Beyond · Sketchy · Osmosis · Wikipedia · random articles · school lectures · unapproved textbooks · UpToDate (only with verification against local guidelines)

## Images and copyright

Only use images that are **public domain or carry an accepted [Creative Commons licence](https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/cclicenses/)**. When unsure, ask us.

Every image needs a credit — *italicised, font size 10pt*, in this format:

!!! example "Credit format"
    Author name (hyperlinked to source), Creative Commons/Public Domain (hyperlinked to the CC licence), via website/journal name

??? note "Three ways to format the credit"
